Broken code goes in sealed.
A verified fix comes out.

Sealvix diagnoses, patches and re-verifies your repo inside a throwaway gVisor sandbox on Vultr. Every step gets a fresh container, no network, no secrets, and is destroyed after. You get the diff, the before/after logs, and a SHA-256 receipt.
